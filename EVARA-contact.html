FAQ

Frequently asked questions

A few things clients often ask before we begin.

Every project starts with a conversation about your site, how you live and what you hope the space can become. We then visit the site and study its climate and context before shaping a first concept.

We design residential homes, interiors and hospitality spaces, as well as renovation and adaptive reuse. Each project is approached through climate-responsive and sustainable design.

It means the building works with sun, wind, rain and shade first: orientation, overhangs, cross-ventilation and natural light, so spaces stay comfortable with less reliance on mechanical systems.

The studio is based in Kerala, India, and our approach adapts to projects across diverse contexts. Tell us where your site is and we’ll discuss how we can work together.

It depends on the scale and type of project. After our first conversation and site visit, we share a clear timeline covering concept, design development, documentation and construction support.
