ABOUT EVARA

Architecture that begins with the environment.

At Evara Architecture Studio, we believe meaningful architecture begins with an understanding of the environment. Our approach is rooted in climate-responsive and sustainable design, creating spaces that respond to their surroundings, work with nature, and enhance the way people live.

THE FOUNDER

Ar. Anakha P

Architect / Environmental Designer

Anakha P is an architect and environmental designer with a background in architectural practice, sustainable design, and environmental research. Her professional experience spans architectural design, residential projects, interiors, and hospitality spaces, with a particular interest in climate-responsive and context-driven design.

Her postgraduate specialisation in Environmental Design has further strengthened her approach to integrating environmental performance, materiality, passive strategies, and local context into architectural design.

HER APPROACH

Architecture is a dialogue between people, place, climate and material.

Anakha believes that good design should respond to its environmental and cultural context rather than follow a predetermined style. Her approach combines functional planning, passive environmental strategies, material sensitivity and a strong connection to the local character of a place.

Through EVARA, she aims to create architecture that is thoughtful, resource-conscious, climate-responsive and rooted in everyday experience.

  1. PeopleFunctional planning around how you live
  2. PlaceThe local character and culture of a site
  3. ClimatePassive strategies before machines
  4. MaterialHonest, local and sensitively chosen

OUR VISION

Sustainability is not an addition to architecture. It is its foundation.

We envision a built environment where sustainability is not an addition to architecture, but its foundation. Our goal is to create spaces that minimise environmental impact, respond intelligently to local climatic conditions, and embrace the inherent potential of nature.

By combining contextual understanding with contemporary design thinking, Evara aspires to shape a more responsible and resilient future, one where architecture coexists with nature rather than competing against it.
